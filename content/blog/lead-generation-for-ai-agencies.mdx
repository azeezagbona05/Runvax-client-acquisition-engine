Lead generation for an AI agency comes down to five decisions: pick one vertical, define the operational pain you solve in one sentence, build a list of businesses showing that pain, run outbound across email and WhatsApp with a 4–5 touch follow-up over about three weeks, and track every prospect through a pipeline so nothing goes cold. Agencies that skip the vertical decision and pitch "AI automation" to everyone get the reply rates — under 4% — that signal broken targeting.

This is part of the [AI for client acquisition pillar](/blog/ai-for-client-acquisition-guide) and follows [how to find businesses that need AI automation](/blog/finding-businesses-that-need-ai-automation). The general agency version is [lead generation for agencies](/blog/lead-generation-for-agencies); this one is specific to selling AI.

## Decision 1: One Vertical

An AI agency that serves "any business" has no referral network, no reusable demo, and no credibility shortcut. One vertical gives you all three. Fastest-ROI options in 2026: e-commerce operations, B2B SaaS sales ops, HR and recruitment, and customer support. For local services, dental, legal, real estate, and hospitality are less competitive to reach and have visible buying signals.

## Decision 2: The One-Sentence Pain

Write the sentence a prospect would nod at: "You lose enquiries that come in after 6pm." "Your team re-keys every order between the store and the fulfilment system." If you cannot write it, you do not know your offer well enough to sell it cold.

## Decision 4: The Outbound Motion

- **Channels:** email plus WhatsApp where locally common, plus a call for warm-but-quiet leads. Multi-channel produces around 287% more leads than single-channel.
- **Cadence:** 4–5 touches over ~21 days. Vary the angle each time; do not just "check in".
- **Opener:** name the pain and the moment, not the technology.
- **Volume target:** enough that your reply rate (aim 8%+) produces the number of discovery calls your close rate needs.

## Decision 5: The Pipeline

Track every prospect through stages — found, contacted, replied, call booked, proposal, won or lost — with a follow-up flag on anything untouched for three days. Most AI agency revenue is lost not on the pitch but in the gap between a warm reply and the next message. Modern pipelines run the full lifecycle, not just a contact list.
