# Questions for Nigerian legal counsel: proposed outreach and data-protection obligations

**Prepared by:** engineering, for the business owner to send to counsel. **This is not legal advice and states no conclusion.** Every legal question below is **open**. Where we describe the law or a case, it is from **secondary commentary we have not verified against primary sources**, and counsel is asked to correct it. Nothing here should be read as settling any question.

**Entity:** Harzotech Nig Ltd (as named in the privacy policy effective 14 May 2025; to be confirmed). **System:** RUNVAX Industry Acquisition Engine, an admin-only tool, currently local only and not deployed or used on any real clinic.

## 1. What is proposed (facts)

**Pilot:** about 10 dental clinics in Lagos, **emailed by a named person, one at a time, by hand**, from the owner's mailbox. RUNVAX writes a draft from fixed templates; a human reviews, approves and sends it **outside** the system.

| Topic | Fact |
|---|---|
| Recipients | A **role inbox published by the clinic** (for example `info@` or `reception@`) on its own website domain. Named individuals are not used in the pilot |
| Source of the clinic list | An independent list provided by the owner. **Not** Google Maps or Places |
| What we record about a clinic | Business name; website; area; the inbox and business phone it published; observations about its public website (for example "no online booking found") with the page and date; a deterministic score; the admin's notes. **No patient data is intended** |
| Personal data | Possibly the inbox or phone, if it identifies an individual (for example a sole practitioner). **Whether it is personal data is Question B2** |
| Automation | **None.** No automatic sending, no automated follow-up, no AI, no website crawling, and no Google data in the pilot |
| Opt-out | Every pitch ends with an opt-out line. An opt-out or objection **blocks the clinic and its domain** in the system (stored as a keyed hash, not the plain address) |
| Records | An audit trail of who approved what and when; every reply, opt-out and meeting is recorded by hand |
| Hosting | Application on Vercel; the database host appears to be in the UK (`mysql.uk.cloudlogin.co`). **Not verified by engineering** |
| Retention | Provisional periods, **not yet enforced**: see `docs/source-retention-matrix.md` |
| Frequency | Proposed: **one email per clinic, no automatic follow-up; a second contact only after a reply**. *(Owner to confirm.)* |

**Sample pitch** (generated by the tool from observed evidence; clinic details are placeholders; the wording may change after the owner's decisions):

> **Subject:** [Clinic]: a question about patient enquiries and appointment requests
>
> Hello [Clinic] team,
>
> When I looked at [clinic-website] on [date], I couldn't find a way to book an appointment online, and I couldn't find an enquiry form.
>
> Harzotech is preparing an AI Receptionist for dental clinics: it would answer routine patient questions using information the clinic approves, and pass appointment requests to your team to confirm.
>
> Would a short call be useful to see whether this could fit how your clinic works?
>
> Kind regards,
> Harzotech
>
> If you'd prefer not to hear from us, reply STOP and we won't contact you again.

The pitch states **only what was observed**, phrased as what could not be found. It contains no price, figures, guarantees or other clients' results.

## 2. Questions

### A. Lawful basis for the outreach itself
1. **(Q-N2: the central question.)** Can an unsolicited email to a clinic's **role inbox** be sent on the basis of legitimate interest under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, or is **prior consent** required? Does the answer change if the inbox identifies an individual, or if the recipient is a **named** staff member?
2. We have seen secondary commentary on *Onimisi v. Guaranty Trust Holding Co. Plc* (Federal High Court, Abuja, 11 June 2026, as reported), described as finding **no lawful basis for unsolicited marketing to a non-customer with no prior relationship**. **We have not read the judgment.** What does it decide, what is its precedential weight, and **does it reach B2B email to a business inbox, or only consumer marketing**?
3. Is a message that **offers a service** distinguishable from marketing for these purposes? (The pitch makes an observation and offers a call. Counsel's view of how it should be characterised is requested.)
4. Which other Nigerian laws or regulators govern unsolicited commercial email to businesses? We have not identified them; counsel is asked to (Q-N12).

### B. Research and the data we hold
1. **(Q-N1)** Is recording a clinic's published contact points and observations about its public website, before any contact, a legitimate interest under section 25? Is our **purpose, necessity and balancing** analysis (draft LIA, `docs/compliance/dental-pilot-lia.md`) the right shape? **The draft does not conclude it is lawful.**
2. **(Q-N4)** Are role inboxes and business phone numbers of small clinics **personal data**, particularly where a number is the owner's own mobile?
3. **(Q-N3)** Does the NDPC's General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID, as summarised in commentary: issued March 2025, in effect September 2025) require a particular **legitimate-interest assessment** form, and how must it be documented and retained?
4. Is information published on a clinic's own website treated differently as a source for these purposes?

### C. Transparency
1. **(Q-N5)** What notice must be given to people whose data we obtain from a source other than themselves, and **when**? The current privacy policy does **not** mention business research, prospecting or B2B contact data. What must it add (purposes, sources, retention, objection route)?
2. Is the **opt-out line in the email** sufficient notice, or is more required in the first message? Does the **sender identification** (entity, person, postal address) the pitch needs meet any requirement?

### D. Individual rights and records
1. How must an **objection** (section 36, as we understand it) be handled, and how fast? The proposed process: the named person records it the same day, and the clinic and its domain are blocked permanently.
2. If a person asks **where we got their data**, what must we disclose? (The tool records the source, page and date for each fact.)
3. On **erasure**, may we keep a **keyed hash** of the address so we do not contact the person again? (Documented in `docs/suppression-keys.md`.) Does that hash itself remain personal data?
4. Must we keep a **record of processing**, and in what form?

### E. Governance and registration
1. **(Q-N6)** Is a **data protection impact assessment** required for this processing, or for the later outreach phase?
2. **(Q-N8)** Does Harzotech need to register with the NDPC as a controller of major importance, and are audit filings due?
3. Is a **data protection officer** required?

### F. Retention
1. **(Q-N7)** Are our provisional retention periods adequate, and consistent with the privacy policy's stated 24 months for lead records? (Named-contact retention of 180 days without engagement is designed but **not yet enforced**.)
2. Is incidental personal data in short-lived page caches acceptable (Q-N13)? *(Page fetching is switched off in the pilot, so this may not arise.)*

### G. Transfers and hosting
1. **(Q-N9)** The database host appears to be in the **UK** and the application runs on Vercel. What do the NDPA's transfer requirements require, and is anything needed before real data is stored?
2. **(Q-N10)** How should **erasure interact with backups**, and what backup retention is acceptable?
3. No clinic data is sent to any AI provider in the pilot. **If a later phase does this, what is needed first?** *(Asked now so the answer is not a surprise later.)*

### H. Channel scope
1. The pilot is **email only**. We list WhatsApp, SMS and phone for completeness (Q-N11) because the wider product supports WhatsApp: which NCC or other rules would apply if they were ever used?

### I. The pilot as such
1. Does the **small size** of the pilot (10 clinics, manual, one email each) change any of the above, or are the obligations the same?
2. Under what **conditions** would counsel be comfortable with a pilot (for example: consent first, a particular opt-out wording, record-keeping, a stop rule)?
3. What should be done if a clinic **complains**, or the NDPC makes contact?

## 3. What we need back
- A written opinion on A1–A3, stating **conditions** and **changes** required to the pitch, the opt-out wording, the process or the privacy notice.
- A decision on which of Sections B to G must be resolved **before the pilot** and which **before scaling**.
- Suggested wording for the privacy-notice update.
